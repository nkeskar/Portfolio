Scoring Model

Each university is scored across three weighted indices, then ranked by composite score out of 100.

Talent Surplus

Weight: 30% · Higher = Better

Capacity across key academic and research domains.

  • R&D expenditure, endowment, federal funding
  • STEM PhD & graduate population
  • Engineering / CS / life sciences faculty

Commercialization Gap

Weight: 50% · Higher = Worse (flipped)

Translation performance from research to market.

  • Patent filings & citation index
  • SBIR/STTR awards, corporate partnerships
  • Alumni founder density & VC funding

Resource Scarcity

Weight: 20% · Higher = Worse (flipped)

Depth of local ecosystem and institutional support.

  • Innovation center budget, NSF I-Corps
  • VC funding in metro area
  • Distance to nearest tech hub

Universities plotted by Capacity Input (R&D $, patents, talent) against Conversion Efficiency (startups, revenue, VC).
