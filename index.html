PROJECT 04: DCF VALUATION

McDonald's Corporation: DCF Valuation & HOLD Recommendation

Built a full 11-year DCF model for McDonald's (NYSE: MCD) with a 25-year weighted-useful-life depreciation schedule, CAPM-based WACC of 6.31%, and a 2.5% terminal growth rate, arriving at an intrinsic value of $252.43/share against a $304.90 market price, a 20% premium. Benchmarked McDonald's against YUM! Brands on valuation multiples (EV/EBITDA, P/E) and profitability (ROIC, ROA), concluding McDonald's is fairly valued at a HOLD while flagging YUM's negative equity and high leverage as reasons to avoid a new position. Group project, Applied Financial Analysis course, UIUC.
